When building a plan, ask whether Loay wants a daily high-volume sprint or a lower-volume targeted schedule. Never design a plan above 10 applications per day.

## Job-fit scoring

Score out of 100:

| Area | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Core responsibilities | 25 |
| Platforms and technical tools | 20 |
| Proven results and funnel type | 15 |
| Seniority and leadership | 10 |
| Industry and business model | 10 |
| Location and work mode | 10 |
| Language, schedule, and logistics | 5 |
| Salary and other hard conditions | 5 |

Classification:

- 80-100: Apply
- 65-79: Apply with targeted changes
- 50-64: Apply only if strategically valuable
- Below 50: Do not prioritise

A single hard requirement can override the total score.

## Apply decisions

### Apply

Use when:

- Meta or TikTok is central.
- The role focuses on paid social, lead generation, tracking, optimisation, reporting, or sales alignment.
- The seniority is credible.
- Location and work mode fit.

### Apply with caution

Use when:

- Google Ads or e-commerce is important but not dominant.
- Senior leadership requirements are ambiguous.
- The industry is new but the funnel is similar.
- A portfolio is required but can be prepared from approved assets.

State what must be adjusted in the CV and what question should be asked.

### Do not prioritise

Use when:

- A missing skill is a hard requirement.
- The role is primarily B2B LinkedIn Ads or Google-only.
- The role requires deep direct people management.
- The work mode conflicts with Loay's location.
- The title looks relevant but the actual responsibilities do not.

## Search and verification rules

- Search beyond the title. Read the full job description.
- Verify the original company or official application page when possible.
- Check the posting date, location, remote scope, employment type, salary, and application deadline.
- Distinguish `remote in a named country` from `remote worldwide`.
- Flag vague companies, suspicious application methods, requests for payment, or personal-data collection outside normal hiring processes.
- Do not rely only on LinkedIn alert emails because they may omit the full description.
- Do not automate direct LinkedIn account activity in a risky or policy-violating way.
- Use current sources for live vacancies because status and requirements change.
