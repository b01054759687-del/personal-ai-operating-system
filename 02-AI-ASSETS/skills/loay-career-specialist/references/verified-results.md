## Automation and reporting outcomes

- A zero-cost reporting workflow avoided or saved more than EGP 1.5 million annually.
- CAPI-related tracking and optimisation was associated with a 20%-30% reduction in CPL or CPQL.
- HubSpot and reporting workflows improved lead routing, follow-up visibility, and marketing-to-sales reporting.

Clarify the exact comparison period if an interviewer asks how the percentage was calculated.

## Metric interpretation

An established interview story is that PlanSee initially focused on CPL, while Loay identified CPQL as a more useful measure of campaign quality.

Use the story in this sequence:

1. The business was evaluating performance mainly through CPL.
2. Loay noticed that cheap leads were not always qualified.
3. He recommended tracking CPQL and downstream outcomes.
4. Reporting was aligned more closely with meetings, deals, and lead quality.
5. Decisions became better connected to business outcomes rather than lead volume alone.

Do not say Loay single-handedly changed the entire company unless he confirms that level of ownership.

These figures may represent a later or broader time window, but their precise scope is not stored here. Treat them as `unverified for automatic use`.

Do not include any of these expanded figures in a CV, cover letter, application, portfolio, interview answer, or assessment unless Loay confirms:

- Exact date range
- Definitions

- Whether March 2026 or later months are included

## Rules for using numbers

### Performance CV

Prioritise:

- EGP 2.29 million spend
- 1,982 qualified leads
- 392 meetings
- 72 deals
- 13,377 sqm
- 20%-30% improvement

### Senior Marketing Specialist CV

Balance campaign results with:

- CRM automation
- Reporting workflow
- Sales alignment
- Cross-functional coordination
- Acting leadership
- Annual cost avoidance or savings

### Cover letters and recruiter messages

Use one or two numbers only. Choose the figures most relevant to the job. Do not paste the full metric set into a short message.

### LinkedIn

Use a compact result statement with sufficient context. Avoid a wall of numbers.

### Portfolio

Show:

- Objective
- Initial problem
- Data and tracking setup
- Strategy
- Tests
- Results
- Business interpretation
- Limitations

Remove confidential internal data, personal customer data, account IDs, exact internal processes, and anything PlanSee has not approved for external use.

### Interview

Explain:

- What the baseline was
- What Loay personally did
- What other teams did
- What changed
- How the result was measured
- What remained outside Loay's control

### Assessment

Do not use PlanSee results as guaranteed benchmarks for another business. They can support credibility or illustrate a method, but forecasts must be based on the new industry, audience, funnel, offer, creative quality, customer support, budget, and tracking.

## Safe interview stories

### Story 1: Lead quality over cheap leads

- Situation: CPL was the main measure.
- Task: Improve the quality of marketing decisions.
- Action: Introduced stronger CPQL and downstream reporting.
- Result: Better visibility into qualified leads, meetings, and deals.

### Story 2: Tracking and CAPI

- Situation: Platform and CRM data did not provide sufficient optimisation visibility.
- Task: Improve tracking quality and reduce inefficient acquisition.
- Action: Supported CAPI and CRM-connected workflows.
- Result: Reported 20%-30% CPL or CPQL improvement.

### Story 3: Reporting workflow

- Situation: A costly reporting option or manual process was being considered.
- Task: Create a practical alternative.
- Action: Built a zero-cost reporting workflow.
- Result: More than EGP 1.5 million in annual avoided cost or savings.

### Story 4: Acting leadership

- Situation: Daily work needed clearer ownership and deadlines.
- Task: Improve coordination without an official management title.
- Action: Used Trello, a RACI matrix, and task deadlines.
- Result: Clearer responsibilities and follow-up.

For every story, use only the level of ownership Loay can defend in a follow-up question.
