## Interview style

- Give the direct answer first.
- Use simple English that Loay can say naturally.
- Keep most answers between 45 and 90 seconds.
- Use a verified example instead of generic theory.
- Distinguish Loay's work from team or company work.
- Do not memorise every word. Memorise the sequence and evidence.

Hi, my name is Loay Ashraf Mostafa.

I am currently a Senior Marketing Specialist at PlanSee. I joined the company as a Marketing Specialist and was promoted after taking more ownership of performance marketing, lead generation, reporting, and CRM automation.

My main experience is with Meta and TikTok campaigns, including campaign setup, audience research, A/B testing, retargeting, budget optimisation, creative analysis, and performance reporting. I also work closely with sales and internal teams to improve lead quality and follow-up visibility.

Shorten it when the requested recording has a strict time limit.

## Common questions

### Tell me about yourself

Use the voice introduction. Adapt the final sentence to the vacancy.

### Why are you looking for a new role?

```text
I have gained strong hands-on experience at PlanSee and progressed to Senior Marketing Specialist. I am now looking for a role where I can work on a wider range of campaigns, learn from a larger marketing environment, and continue growing in performance marketing and media buying.
```

Do not criticise PlanSee.

### How do you evaluate campaign performance?

Discuss:

- Business objective
- Funnel stage
- Tracking quality
- CPM, CTR, CPC, landing-page or lead-form conversion
- CPL
- CPQL
- Meetings
- Deals
- Revenue or commercial outcome when attribution permits

Explain that the cheapest lead is not always the best lead.

### How do you stay current?

Use:

- Official Meta, TikTok, and relevant platform updates
- Trusted newsletters and resources
- Competitor and market monitoring
- Hands-on testing
- Real campaign data

Do not claim Google Ads expertise merely because official Google updates are followed.

### How do you work with sales?

Explain:

- Shared lead definitions
- Lead-routing visibility
- Follow-up feedback
- Qualified-lead measurement
- Meetings and deals
- Reporting that connects marketing activity to business outcomes

### Tell me about leadership

Say that Loay acted in a team-leadership capacity during daily execution. Explain Trello, RACI, deadlines, and follow-up. State clearly that it was not an official Team Leader title if asked.

## Questions to ask the interviewer

Choose two or three:

- What are the main business goals for this role in the first three months?
- Which platforms and markets will be the main focus?
- How does the team currently define a qualified lead or successful conversion?
- How are marketing results connected with sales or CRM data?
- What are the biggest challenges in the current campaigns?
- How is the marketing team structured, and who would this role work with most closely?

## Marketing assessment order

Always use this sequence unless the brief explicitly requires another:

1. Clarify the business objective and constraints.
2. Research the industry, market, competitors, product, offer, funnel, and customer behaviour.
3. Identify audience segments.
4. Prioritise the largest, most reachable, or easiest-to-convert audience for the first phase.
5. Select platforms based on audience presence, intent, funnel role, creative requirements, and economics.
6. Allocate budget or media distribution.
7. Define campaign structure.
8. Define creative and content-testing approach.
9. Define tracking, CRM, lead-quality, and customer-support processes.
10. Establish benchmarks.
11. Build scenarios and forecasts.
12. Define KPIs, reporting, optimisation, and scaling rules.

Audience analysis must come before platform selection and budget distribution.

## Research requirements

Research should cover:

- Industry size and dynamics
- Customer needs and objections
- Competitors and positioning
- Price and offer
- Buying cycle
- Geographic scope
- Platform behaviour
- Creative conventions
- Funnel and conversion path
- Sales or customer-support capacity
- Tracking quality
- Available budget and time

Do not select a platform simply because Loay has used it before.

## Platform and campaign structure

For each recommended platform, explain:

- Why the audience is there
- The platform's role in the funnel
- Campaign objective
- Audience type
- Creative format
- Budget share
- Test structure
- KPI
- Optimisation rule

Campaign structure must follow the business, audience, data volume, and budget. Avoid unnecessary complexity.

## Benchmarks and forecasting

Treat benchmarks as directional references, not promises.

State:

- Source or basis
- Market and date
- Funnel stage
- Assumptions
- Low, base, and high scenario when possible

Forecasts depend on:

- Content and creative quality
- Offer and pricing
- Audience accuracy
- Landing page or lead form
- Sales follow-up
- Customer-support speed and quality
- Budget
- Seasonality
- Competition
- Tracking quality
- Website or checkout experience
- Product availability

Never guarantee leads, revenue, ROAS, CPL, or conversion rate before testing.

## Assessment quality check

Before submission:

- Every recommendation connects to research.
- Audience comes before platform and budget.
- Platform roles are clear.
- Budget allocation has a reason.
- Campaign structure fits the budget and data volume.
- Benchmarks are sourced and dated.
- Forecasts show assumptions and dependencies.
- Content and customer support are included as performance variables.
- Tracking and CRM are addressed.
- KPIs cover both platform and business outcomes.
- Confidential employer data is removed.
- The final file answers every item in the original brief.
