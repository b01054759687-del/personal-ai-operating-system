## Tone and language

- Explain strategy to Loay in Egyptian Arabic.
- Write external professional messages in simple English unless Arabic is requested.
- Sound polite, confident, interested, and human.
- Keep LinkedIn and WhatsApp messages shorter than emails.
- Avoid overly formal vocabulary, long apologies, desperation, pressure, or defensive explanations.
- Use `response`, not `respond`, when a noun is required.

## General message rules

Every message should do only what is needed:

1. Acknowledge the person or update.
2. Answer the question directly.
3. Give the required detail.
4. State the next step or ask one clear question.

Do not add Loay's entire background to a scheduling message.

## Interview invitation

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for reaching out. I would be happy to discuss the [Role Title] position and share more about my experience.

Please let me know the available times, and I will confirm what works best.

Best,
Loay
```

## Availability

Historical pattern only:

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for your message. I am available from 5:00 PM onwards on [days], and I am flexible throughout Friday and Saturday.

Please let me know what time works best for you.

Best,
Loay
```

Ask Loay for current dates, times, and timezone before using it.

## Late reply with documents

```text
Hello [Name],

Thank you for your message, and apologies for the late response. I have just seen it.

I have attached my CV and [portfolio, if confirmed] here. I am available [confirmed availability].

Please let me know what time suits you best.

Best,
Loay
```

Do not mention a portfolio unless it is ready and attached.

```text
Best,
Loay
```

## Interview confirmation

```text
Hi [Name],

Just confirming that I will attend the interview today at 12:00 PM, inshallah.

Thank you, and see you then.
```

Use the actual confirmed date and time.

## Rescheduling

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for arranging the interview. Unfortunately, today is not suitable for me.

I am available tomorrow at any time that works for you. Please feel free to choose the most convenient time.

Best,
Loay
```

If a reason is necessary, keep it brief. Do not overexplain.

## Post-call thank-you

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the call today. I appreciated the opportunity to learn more about the role and discuss my experience.

I remain very interested and look forward to the next steps.

Best,
Loay
```

## Follow-up

```text
Hi [Name],

I hope you are doing well. I wanted to follow up regarding the [Role Title] position and ask whether there are any updates on the next steps.

I remain interested in the opportunity.

Best,
Loay
```

Follow up after a reasonable period. Avoid sending repeated daily messages.

## Assessment deadline clarification

If instructions say `ASAP` or `preferably` without a clear deadline:

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for sharing the assessment. As it is a detailed task that requires market, audience, platform, and benchmark research, could you please confirm the exact submission deadline and time?

This will help me plan the work and deliver a complete assessment.

Best,
Loay
```

## Assessment scope and confirmed delivery

For the assessment discussed in July 2026:

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the clarification. I will complete and submit the full assessment by 28 July.

My approach will begin with industry and market research, followed by audience identification and prioritisation. Based on that, I will select the most suitable platforms, define the budget distribution and campaign structure, and develop benchmark-based forecasts with the relevant assumptions.

Best,
Loay
```

Do not use `28 July` for another task without confirmation.

```text
Best,
Loay
```

## Rejection or poor interview

If an interview was extremely short or the interviewer repeatedly closed down answers:

- Do not accuse the interviewer.
- Send a calm thank-you.
- If appropriate, add one sentence reinforcing the strongest relevant experience.
- Continue the search rather than chasing an unresponsive employer repeatedly.

Example:

```text
Hi [Name],

Thank you for your time today. I remain interested in the role and would be happy to provide any additional details about my Meta and TikTok campaign experience, CRM automation, and performance reporting.

Best,
Loay
```

If Loay missed a step or responded late, accept responsibility briefly, confirm that the required action is now complete, and ask whether the process can continue. Do not overexplain personal circumstances.
