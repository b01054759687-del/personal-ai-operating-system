## CV system

Maintain two main CV versions only unless a genuinely different career direction is approved.

### Version A: Performance Marketing Specialist / Media Buyer

Use for:

- Performance Marketing Specialist
- Media Buyer
- Performance Media Buyer
- Paid Social Specialist
- Lead Generation Specialist

Emphasise:

- Meta Ads
- TikTok Ads
- Audience research
- A/B testing
- Retargeting
- Budget allocation
- Funnel optimisation
- Tracking
- CRM automation
- Qualified leads
- Meetings and deals
- CPQL and downstream performance

Use the approved April 2025-February 2026 campaign metrics.

### Version B: Senior Marketing Specialist

Use for:

- Senior Marketing Specialist
- Broader integrated-marketing roles
- Marketing operations roles with substantial paid-media work

Emphasise:

- Paid-media ownership
- Cross-functional coordination
- CRM and reporting workflows
- Sales alignment
- Performance analysis and decision-making
- Acting team leadership
- Trello, RACI, and deadlines
- Cost avoidance or savings

Do not turn this version into a generic brand or content-marketing CV. Keep performance marketing visible.

## ATS tailoring

For each vacancy:

1. Extract repeated hard skills, platforms, funnel types, industry terms, responsibilities, seniority signals, and outcome metrics.
2. Map each keyword to verified evidence.
3. Add exact job-description wording only when it truthfully describes Loay's work.
4. Put high-value keywords in the headline, summary, skills, and recent experience where natural.
5. Remove irrelevant details before adding more words.
6. Keep formatting simple and ATS-readable.

Never:

- Add unsupported tools
- Copy an entire job description
- Use hidden keywords
- Inflate years of experience
- Change internship work into full ownership
- Turn acting leadership into an official manager title
- Claim direct revenue causation without qualification

## Bullet-writing rules

Use:

`Action + scope + method + measured result + business meaning`

Example:

`Managed Meta and TikTok campaigns across a EGP 2.29M budget, generating 1,982 qualified leads, 392 campaign-attributed meetings, and 72 deals between April 2025 and February 2026.`

Use `campaign-attributed` where attribution is not fully causal.

Keep four to six strong bullets for the latest role. Prioritise results over duty lists.

## Summary rules

Keep the summary to three or four lines.

Include:

- Target title
- Hands-on platforms
- Funnel or business type
- Strongest differentiators
- One compact result signal

Do not begin with adjectives such as `dynamic`, `passionate`, or `results-driven` without evidence.

Default conservative experience wording:

`Performance marketing specialist with 2+ years of hands-on experience in paid social, lead generation, CRM automation, and campaign analysis.`

## LinkedIn optimisation

Optimise:

- Headline
- About
- Current and previous experience
- Skills
- Featured section
- Contact information
- Open-to-work preferences

Suggested headline:

`Performance Marketing Specialist | Media Buyer | Meta & TikTok Ads | Lead Generation | CRM Automation`

About structure:

1. Current identity and focus
2. Platforms and funnel experience
3. One or two business outcomes
4. Collaboration and automation strengths
5. Target opportunity and contact invitation

Use recruiter-search keywords naturally. Avoid keyword stuffing.

## Cover letters

- Keep cover letters brief.
- Make them sound like a real person, not AI-generated.
- Start with role-specific relevance.
- Use one strong achievement.
- Explain one reason for interest.
- End with a clear invitation to discuss.
- Do not repeat the entire CV.

Typical length:

- 150-250 words for email or formal application
- 80-150 words when the platform encourages brevity

## Quality control

Before finalising a CV or profile:

- Check name, phone, email, dates, and title consistency.
- Check `2+` versus `3+` years.
- Check that March 2026 metrics are excluded unless approved.
- Check that WDS is described as an internship and part-time/project-based when overlap matters.
- Check that leadership wording is honest.
- Check that every metric has a clear date range and definition.
- Check that the document targets one role family.
- Check that the strongest evidence appears on the first page.
